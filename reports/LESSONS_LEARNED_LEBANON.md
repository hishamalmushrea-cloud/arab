# Lessons Learned — Lebanon

- Current nine-governorate law and historical eight-governorate survey frame must coexist.
- Keserwan and Jbeil have one current parent: Keserwan-Jbeil.
- Mount Lebanon remains their historical survey parent only through Claims.
- Two current administrative-parent relationships are invalid even if one is labelled historical.
- Current district denominator remains 26 while parent assignment changes.
- Municipalities require a current Ministry register and are not inferred from district reports.
