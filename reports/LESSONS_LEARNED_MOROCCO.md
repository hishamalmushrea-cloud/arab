# Lessons Learned — Morocco

- The official 75 universe is 13 prefectures plus 62 provinces.
- The 83 figure includes prefectures of arrondissements and cannot replace the second-level denominator.
- Prefecture and province need separate entity types even when both are direct region children.
- The known 1,503-commune denominator remains open until record-level import is accepted.
- HCP RGPH 2024 is a population cross-check, not a substitute for legal hierarchy identity.
- Exact French official forms remain canonical where atomic Arabic forms are not yet captured.
