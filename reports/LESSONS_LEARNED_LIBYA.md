# Lessons Learned — Libya

- The current Ministry page yields 141 exact municipality names despite skipped display numbers 124 and 140.
- Display labels are not denominators; record count comes from exact names.
- The earlier 93 records were a bounded subset and cannot remain the national denominator after a complete source snapshot is captured.
- Historical 2006 sha‘biyat remain separate and never parent current municipalities.
- Catalogue presence does not establish effective control.
- Alternate-authority lists require separate dated overlays, not a silent union.
