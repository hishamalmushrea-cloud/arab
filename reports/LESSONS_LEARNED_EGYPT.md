# Lessons Learned — Egypt

- Four governorates are urban-only in the cited CAPMAS profile; 23 have urban/rural geography.
- Governorate is not city, and an urban profile does not create a city entity.
- Markaz/local-unit and qism/shiyakha are parallel lower paths.
- Lower levels must stay unavailable until each profile has an accepted denominator.
- CAPMAS numeric codes and bilingual names prevent alias duplication.
- Cairo and Giza are the required contrasting lower-topology pair for any subsequent depth.
