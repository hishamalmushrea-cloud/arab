# Lessons Learned — Yemen

- A stale official frame can remain valid historical evidence but cannot silently define the present denominator.
- A current universe may be reproducibly reconciled from an exact legacy enumeration plus a later creation law.
- Amanat Al Asimah is a parallel first-level capital municipality, not a governorate alias.
- Socotra requires its own temporal boundary and instrument; its absence from the legacy list is evidence of time, not an error to erase.
- Issuance/establishment evidence must not be expanded into unobserved effective-clause wording.
- A legal/statistical list proves neither effective control nor destruction, displacement, or disputed status.
- Lower-level counts and records remain unavailable until a current coded register is reconciled.
