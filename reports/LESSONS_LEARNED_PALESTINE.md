# Lessons Learned — Palestine

- Sixteen governorates are a PCBS legal/statistical scope, not a statement of effective control.
- West Bank/Gaza profiles remain statistical classifications.
- Destroyed, displaced, and de facto statuses require separately dated atomic evidence.
- Local authorities, localities, and camps are different identities.
- UNESCO freshness changed the property denominator to six in 2026.
- Sebastia's emergency inscription and danger status require explicit dated Claims, not generalized security conclusions.

## Depth cycle 1 — 1948 depopulated localities (2026-08-17)

First Palestine cycle under **Maximum Arabic Knowledge Coverage** — the project's largest single memory-preservation import:

- **584 archive-attested 1948 localities** entered as `historical_place` at `probable`, located in **8 Mandate-era historical qadas** (`ps_historical_qada`, new additive vocab type): صفد 95 · جنين 100 · بئر السبع 93 · حيفا 92 · عكا 73 · الرملة 70 · طبريا 31 · القدس 30.
- **Two frames, never mixed:** the 1948 historical qada frame is entirely separate from the current 16-governorate tree; a gate mutation rejects attaching any site to a current governorate.
- **Identity without condition:** the archive lists include depopulated and still-inhabited sites; per-site destruction/depopulation is **never inferred** — `status=historical` records frame membership, and marking a site `destroyed` without a dated source is a detected mutation.
- **Definition difference preserved:** archive counts every inhabited locality (قرية/خربة/عرب/مزرعة) while WAFA counts administrative villages (e.g. Safad 95 vs 78) — both readings recorded per qada, never reconciled artificially.
- **National frame disputes verbatim:** 531 (PLO) / 530 / ~520 / 775-seized recorded in one unpublished country-level claim; no national denominator adopted.
- **Same-name rule:** الطيرة/المنصورة/المنشية recur across qadas as distinct localities; identity keys carry qada+sequence so automated deduplication can never erase real places.
- Gate: **13/13 mutations** (5 new); independent review **1,292/1,292**.
