# Lessons Learned — Mauritania

- Fifteen wilayas include three Nouakchott wilayas that remain wilayas, not inferred neighborhoods.
- A known 63-moughataa count can remain open until the exact parent register is imported.
- Commune counts 219 and 220 must remain a disputed Claim; neither becomes a denominator by default.
- Older 57-moughataa/220-commune publications cannot override current DGAT/ANSADE evidence.
- Profile closure is separate from lower administrative coverage.
