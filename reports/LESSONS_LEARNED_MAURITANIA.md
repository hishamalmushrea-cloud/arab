# Lessons Learned — Mauritania

- Fifteen wilayas include three Nouakchott wilayas that remain wilayas, not inferred neighborhoods.
- A known 63-moughataa count can remain open until the exact parent register is imported.
- Commune counts 219 and 220 must remain a disputed Claim; neither becomes a denominator by default.
- Older 57-moughataa/220-commune publications cannot override current DGAT/ANSADE evidence.
- Profile closure is separate from lower administrative coverage.

## Depth cycle 1 (2026-09-21)

- A State Party can hold a sole-submission element on the Urgent Safeguarding List (T'heydinn 00524): the `national` classification rests on the submitting-State list, not on how famous the element is.
- Three of the nine inscribed elements are sole submissions and six are shared files; the shared list is asserted exactly as published and no shared element is ever promoted.
- A declared 2026 nomination is neither an inscription nor a denial: it is recorded once, published as a stated nomination, and a mutation fails if the element is written as inscribed.
- Reading a property page for its criteria is part of the citation: the natural property's criteria were not read, so they are recorded empty and no cultural description is built on it.
- The tentative list (2001, three sites) is a separate predicate from inscription; a mutation fails if a tentative site is recorded as an inscribed property.
- The Constitution is the only source that may carry the official-language claim, and the four national languages are published only with a second source from the ISO 639-3 registry; the registry supplies codes, never speaker counts.
- The mirrors print a speaker table and a 2013 population estimate: both are excluded, and every unpublished language row states the no-count rule in the record itself so the omission is visible, not silent.
- A place whose wilaya cannot be verified (كمبي صالح) is recorded at country level rather than guessed into a wilaya, and every heritage place carries one `located_in` relation, no claim and no coordinates.
- UNESCO-sourced places stay `source_verified` while mirror-sourced places stay `local_reported`, so the same layer carries two confidence levels without blending them.
