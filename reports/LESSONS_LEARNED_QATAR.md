# Lessons Learned — Qatar

- Current eight-municipality evidence must not be mixed with historical ten-municipality documentation.
- Municipality, city, numbered zone, block, district, and fareej are separate identities.
- PSA and QNMP provide independent census and planning views of the same eight-name universe.
- Arabic/English spelling variants (`Umm Slal`, `Al Daayen`, `Al Sheehaniya`) are Aliases, not duplicate entities.
- Census reference is end December 2020; retrieval is separate.
- Al Zubarah is an archaeological property associated with Qatar, not a municipality or automatically a city entity.
- Lower layers remain unavailable until a current enumerated topology is accepted.
