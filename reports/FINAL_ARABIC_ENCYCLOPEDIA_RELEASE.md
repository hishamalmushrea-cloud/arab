# Final Arabic Encyclopedia Structured Release

## Decision

# `COMPLETE WITH DOCUMENTED LIMITATIONS`

## Basis
All 22 country manifests now have accepted Schema 2.0.0 structured coverage. The four original pilots and eighteen production cycles passed schema, integrity, provenance, denominator, freshness, idempotence, independent-review, negative-test, preservation, generation, local gate, and remote CI requirements at their release commits.

## Meaning of this decision
The release is complete for its accepted bounded country scopes. It is not a claim that every city, village, neighborhood, historical site, cultural practice, dialect form, or conflict-status overlay has been documented.

## Closed national entry layers
Every country has a verified country root and at least one accepted bounded administrative or equivalent national entry layer. Parallel structures, historical frames, proposed transitions, special-capital paths, and authority-specific narratives remain distinct where required.

## Documented limitations
- Lower administrative layers remain open or denominator-unavailable in multiple countries.
- National populated-place, village, neighborhood, quarter, block, fareej, mahalla, and harat coverage is not universally complete.
- Cultural and dialect layers are evidence-shaped and frequently deferred; no template completion is claimed.
- Legal identity is separated from de facto control, destruction, displacement, dispute, and access; many such overlays remain unimported.
- Yemen's complete official-gazette Socotra text, Sudan's live Presidency catalogue page, and several lower registers remain source-access limitations.
- Country closeouts and `reports/EXPANSION_LESSONS.md` are the authoritative limitation ledger.

## Quality boundary
No overall completion percentage is asserted. Percentages apply only to named layers with dated denominators. Unknown denominators remain null with a missing reason.

## Source of truth
JSON/JSONL and schemas under `data/` and `schema/` are authoritative. Markdown, HTML, and CSV are generated views.

## Continuing maintenance
Future work is maintenance and documented depth expansion: source freshness, legal transitions, accepted lower registers, evidence-backed places, cultural Claims, dialect attestations, and dated conflict overlays. Each addition remains subject to the same country gates and anti-fabrication rules.
