# Lessons Learned — Syria

- Current institutional navigation can provide an exact upper-layer enumeration when reconciled with a separate official count and hierarchy source.
- Historical statistical continuity is useful but never proves current authority or territorial control.
- Damascus Governorate and Rural Damascus Governorate must remain distinct identities.
- A known denominator may remain open: 68 districts and 227 subdistricts are not permission to fabricate record-level lists.
- Legal/institutional identity does not establish effective control, destruction, displacement, dispute, or access.
- Transition-era freshness requires current government evidence while preserving older source dates and roles explicitly.
