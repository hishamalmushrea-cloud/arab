# Lessons Learned — Oman

- Legal effective date and retrieval date are separate.
- Current 63 equals the older 61-code identity universe plus Al Jabal Al Akhdar and Sinaw.
- A stale coded domain can support identity reconciliation but not a current denominator.
- Dhofar exposed a translation defect: Sadah omission and Muqshin/Qishn ambiguity required annex/current-source reconciliation.
- Municipality branches are not wilayats; niyabahs are selective ministerial creations, not a universal third level.
- Full child-set review is practical and preferable for 63 records.
