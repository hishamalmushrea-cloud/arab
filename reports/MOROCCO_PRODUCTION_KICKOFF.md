# Morocco Production Expansion — Micro Pilot Kickoff

## Status
**STARTED — research only; no authoritative Morocco production records emitted.**

## Mode
Micro Pilot because the second level contains parallel prefectures and provinces, followed by a high-volume commune layer.

## Initial evidence
- DGCT states 12 regions, 75 prefectures/provinces, and 1,503 communes.
- Decree 2.15.10 of 20 February 2015 enumerates the 12 regions, capitals, and constituent prefectures/provinces.
- HCP RGPH 2024 publishes legal population by region, province/prefecture, and commune.

## Principal discrepancy
A DGCT capacity page mentions 83 when prefectures of arrondissements are included, while the official territorial headline is 75 prefectures/provinces. These universes must not be merged. The micro pilot will distinguish top-level prefecture/province entities from prefectures of arrondissements before choosing a denominator.

## Planned scope
Close 12 regions first; reconcile all 75 province/prefecture children from Decree 2.15.10; use RGPH 2024 for population/date cross-check. Do not begin 1,503 communes until the second-level distinction and importer are accepted.
