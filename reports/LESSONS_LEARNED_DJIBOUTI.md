# Lessons Learned — Djibouti

- Five decentralized regions are parallel to special-status Djibouti City.
- Ras-Dika, Boulaos, and Balbala are communes under the special city, not regions.
- Regional sub-prefectures are state territorial units, not city communes.
- Provisional census populations must retain provisional status and reconcile to the national total.
- French source forms remain canonical when no atomic official Arabic form is available.
- Zero inscribed UNESCO properties does not mean cultural absence; tentative sites remain tentative.

## Depth cycle 1 lessons (2026-09-21)

7. A State Party can hold **three** elements at once and the three are not the same kind of thing: one is a **sole-State Urgent Safeguarding** file (Xeedho 02001), one is a **three-State** Representative List file (Xeer Ciise 02087), and one is a **seven-State** file already published for Comoros (the zaffa 02283). The validator pins each reference to its own classification, year and co-submitting-State count, so the same country can neither claim exclusivity on a shared file nor flatten a national one.
8. A **pending nomination is not an inscription**: the 2026 Afar Madqa is published as a stated nomination with an explicit note, and two mutations fail if it is unpublished or dressed up as an inscribed element.
9. **Sign languages without a registry entry stay without a code.** `dgs` is Dogoso, not Djibouti Sign Language; rather than inventing a code, the claim records the sign language as reported, unpublished, with an empty code list — and mutations fail if a code or a publication is added.
10. The mirror's **speaker counts are deliberately not recorded**: the no-numbers rule is enforced in three places (semantic validator, negative suite, independent review) on the payload, the notes and the whole claim blob.
11. **Two count systems can coexist honestly**: the mirror's 35 sub-prefectures for the city and the roadmap's 13 national sub-prefectures are reconciled by recording the discrepancy as an unpublished note and keeping the accepted value authoritative.
12. Heritage places inside the capital attach with **one `located_in` to the city only** — no commune inference, no claim, no population — even though the three communes are accepted administrative children of the same city.
13. A 1999 press report can still be useful when it is dated, tier-B and clearly parted from the UNESCO element it resembles: the rope-tied container ritual is recorded as a local narrative next to the Xeedho element, never as the element itself.
