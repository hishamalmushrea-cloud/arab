# Lessons Learned — Djibouti

- Five decentralized regions are parallel to special-status Djibouti City.
- Ras-Dika, Boulaos, and Balbala are communes under the special city, not regions.
- Regional sub-prefectures are state territorial units, not city communes.
- Provisional census populations must retain provisional status and reconcile to the national total.
- French source forms remain canonical when no atomic official Arabic form is available.
- Zero inscribed UNESCO properties does not mean cultural absence; tentative sites remain tentative.
