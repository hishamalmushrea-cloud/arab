# Lessons Learned — Algeria

- Current coverage remains 58 even when a law announces a future 69-unit universe.
- Eleven future wilayas require `proposed`, `valid_from: 2027-01-01`, and a separate planning denominator.
- A complete future planning registry does not make future units current.
- The 1,541-commune count is stable across the transition but record import remains open.
- Daïras are state administrative extensions and must not be conflated with territorial collectivities.
- Current/future status, date, claims, relationships, denominators, and coverage all need synchronized mutations.
