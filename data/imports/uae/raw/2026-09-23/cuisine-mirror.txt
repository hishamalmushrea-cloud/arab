Emirati cuisine (English mirror). Emirati cuisine is the local traditional Arabic cuisine of the United Arab Emirates; it is part of Eastern Arabian cuisine. Dishes that are often eaten in the UAE include maqlūba, harīs, mačbūs, frsee'ah, fireed, jisheid, and mašwi. Breakfast usually features breads like ragāg, khameer, and chebab, served with cheese, date syrup, or eggs. Balaleat is another dish ... introduced with pasta. Sweet options include luqaimat, a deep-fried ball of pancake batter rolled in sesame seeds and drizzled with date syrup, served with Arabic coffee. The dishes are usually like stews, as everything is often cooked in a single pot; saffron, cardamom, turmeric and thyme are core flavours. Meat from animals of the Arabian Peninsula and fish of the Gulf, plus camel milk and dates, form the base; camel meat is normally reserved for special occasions.
