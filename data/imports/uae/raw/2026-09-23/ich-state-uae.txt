United Arab Emirates — UNESCO Intangible Cultural Heritage (state page).
Ratification of the Convention on 2 May 2005. Listing of ICH and Register: 21 elements inscribed.
Al Azi, art of performing praise, pride and fortitude poetry (2017, USL).
Registered in 2025: Safeguarding programme for Al Sadu, traditional weaving skills in the United Arab Emirates (Art18).
Elements: Al-Taghrooda, traditional Bedouin chanted poetry in the United Arab Emirates and the Sultanate of Oman (2012, RL); Al-Ayyala, a traditional performing art of the Sultanate of Oman and the United Arab Emirates (2014, RL); Majlis, a cultural and social space (2015, RL); Al-Razfa, a traditional performing art (2015, RL); Camel racing, a social practice and a festive heritage associated with camels (2020, RL); Al Aflaj, traditional irrigation network system in the UAE (2020, RL); Falconry, a living human heritage (2021, RL); Arabic calligraphy: knowledge, skills and practices (2021, RL); Al Talli, traditional embroidery skills in the United Arab Emirates (2022, RL); Alheda'a, oral traditions of calling camel flocks (2022, RL); Date palm, knowledge, skills, traditions and practices (2022, RL); Harees dish: know-how, skills and practices (2023, RL); Arabic coffee, a symbol of generosity (2024, RL); Henna: rituals, aesthetic and social practices (2024, RL); Al Sadu, traditional weaving skills in the United Arab Emirates (2025, RL); Bisht (men's Abaa): skills and practices (2025, RL); Arabic Kohl (2025, RL); Al Ahalla, a living performing art in the United Arab Emirates (2025, RL); The zaffa in the traditional wedding (2025, RL).
On-going nomination(s) 2026: Oud instrument: practices, skills and performing arts (RL); Al Saafiyat and plant fibers: craft and social traditions (RL); Harees dish: know-how, skills and practices (RL); Henna: rituals, aesthetic and social practices (RL).
