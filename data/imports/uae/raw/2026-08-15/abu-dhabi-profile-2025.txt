Title: Abu Dhabi
Publisher: The Official Platform of the UAE Government
Relevant exact excerpts:
The three main regions of the emirate are: the city of Abu Dhabi; Al Ain in the east; Al Dhafrah in the west.
The emirate is rich in natural wonders, including the famous scenic Liwa oasis in the south of the emirate, which is home to some of the largest sand dunes in the world. Also, Al Ain region ... is located in the foothills of Jebel Hafeet, and is home to several heritage sites.
