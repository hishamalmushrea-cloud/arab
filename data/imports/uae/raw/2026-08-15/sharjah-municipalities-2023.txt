Title: SCC’s Committee completes visits to Sharjah municipalities
Publisher: Sharjah Government Media Bureau
Published: Thursday 19 October 2023
Relevant exact excerpt:
The committee visited citizen registration sites in the electoral bodies in all municipalities of the Emirate of Sharjah, including the Municipality of Sharjah City, the Municipality of Al-Hamriyah Region, the Municipality of Al-Dhaid City, the Municipality of Mleiha Region, the Municipality of Al-Madam Region, the Municipality of Al-Batayeh Region, the Municipality of Dibba Al-Hisn City, and the Municipality of Kalba City and the municipality of Khorfakkan city.
