Ayala or the 'stick dance' is one of the folk dance forms. It involves performances by two rows of dancers facing one another.
Today, Emiratis play traditional music and perform traditional dances on important social occasions.
The Emirati traditional form of weaving, known as Sadu, is on the United Nations Educational, Scientific and Cultural Organization's 'List of Intangible Cultural Heritage in Need of Urgent Safeguarding'.
