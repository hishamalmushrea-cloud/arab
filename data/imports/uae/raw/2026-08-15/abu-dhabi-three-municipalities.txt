Title: Occupational Safety & Health Management System
Publisher: Department of Municipalities and Transport, Abu Dhabi
Relevant exact excerpt:
The Sector Regulatory Authority (SRA) for Building and Construction Sector is the Department of Municipalities and Transport (DMT). The three municipalities (Abu Dhabi City Municipality, Al Ain City Municipality, and Al Dhafra Region Municipality) are considered as Sub-Sector Regulatory Authorities (SSRA) under supervision of the Department of Municipalities and Transport (DMT).
The page also refers to the geographical zone jurisdiction of Abu Dhabi City Municipality.
