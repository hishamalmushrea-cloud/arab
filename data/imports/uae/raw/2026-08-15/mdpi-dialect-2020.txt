Title: Promoting Arabic Literacy in Primary Schools in the United Arab Emirates through the Emirati Dialect
Author: Jean Pierre Ribeiro Daquila
Journal: Sci 2020, 2(4), 93
DOI: 10.3390/sci2040093
Published: 18 December 2020
Relevant exact Chapter XXI comparison evidence recorded by the pilot:
وايد — “very much/a lot”; identified as Khaleeji and shared beyond the UAE.
شو — “what”; identified as Levantine and Khaleeji.
ربع — “friends”; documented as a colloquial Emirati word.
