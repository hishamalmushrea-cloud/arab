Title: Sharjah
Publisher: The Official Platform of the UAE Government
Relevant exact excerpts:
It is the only emirate that lies on both coasts; the Arabian Gulf in the west and the Gulf of Oman (Indian Ocean) in the east.
The emirate has large desert regions, with some agricultural areas.
Khorfakkan - It is the largest town on the east coast.
Kalba - It maintains a historical charm and is known for its old forts.
Sharjah is a land with architectural heritage and cultural wealth. It is also known for its various touristic sites, which include historical areas like Heart of Sharjah.
