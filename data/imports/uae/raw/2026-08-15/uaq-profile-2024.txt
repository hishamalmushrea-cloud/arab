Title: Umm Al Quwain
Publisher: The Official Platform of the UAE Government
Relevant exact excerpts:
Fishing is a key contributor to the economy of Umm Al Quwain.
The emirate has rich coastal mangroves on the coast of Arabian Gulf and many islands that lie to the east of the mainland.
Traditional activities such as dhow building and sports such as falconry and camel racing are also popular in the emirate.
