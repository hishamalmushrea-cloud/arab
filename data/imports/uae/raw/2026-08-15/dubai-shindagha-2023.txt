Al Shindagha is a historic neighbourhood that sits in the heart of Dubai’s original city centre, built next to Khor Dubai, also known as the Dubai Creek, an important passageway into the city from the sea. It also faces Deira and Bur Dubai, neighbourhoods with similar significance in the city’s history of pearl diving and fishing, trade, cultural expansion, and family heritage.
